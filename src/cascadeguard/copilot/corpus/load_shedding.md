# Controlled load shedding and under-frequency load shedding

## Generation deficit
When a large infeed is lost (for example an interconnection or a large unit) and the remaining generation cannot ramp fast enough, frequency falls. Automatic under-frequency load shedding (UFLS) relays then disconnect blocks of load in stages. UFLS is a last-resort safety net: it acts in fixed blocks, so it usually sheds more load than strictly necessary.

## Controlled shedding
Operator-initiated or scheme-initiated controlled shedding removes only the amount needed, at the locations where it relieves the constraint. Shedding close to the load end of an overloaded radial corridor relieves every section upstream of it.

## Restoration
Shed load should be restored in steps as soon as generation, interconnection or line capacity is available again, checking loading and voltage after each step.
