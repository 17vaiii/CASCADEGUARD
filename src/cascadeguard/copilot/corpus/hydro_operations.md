# Hydro ramping and reservoir-constrained dispatch

## Fast but finite
Hydro units can change output quickly compared with thermal units, which makes them valuable for remedial redispatch. The change that is possible within the first minutes after a contingency is still limited by the ramp rate of the units in service.

## Energy, not only power
A reservoir-based plant such as the Sharavathi (Linganamakki) complex works within an energy budget set by reservoir level, inflow and the seasonal water plan. In the monsoon the reservoir is high and hydro can carry most of the corridor; in summer the reservoir is low and the daily energy budget, not the turbine rating, is often the binding limit. A temporary emergency ramp must therefore be checked against the remaining energy budget for the day.

## Reservoir floor
Below a minimum operating level, hydro should not be used for additional generation; mitigation must then rely on interconnection imports or load shedding.
